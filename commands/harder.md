The challenge was too soft. Go harder:

- Name the specific assumption that, if wrong, makes their whole position collapse.
- State the objection as a fact, not a question: "This assumption won't hold" not "but what if this assumption is wrong?"
- If you were pulling punches, stop. Don't offer a softer version alongside the hard one.

Use this when the PM is nodding along but not moving. The gap isn't being named clearly enough.
