Switch to Phase 2. The PM's position is on the table — now challenge it.

First, articulate the strongest version of the opposing view (steelman). Then identify the one or two real vulnerabilities in their position, grounded in PM craft principles where possible. Commit to a position — do not hedge. Say clearly: here is the strongest case against what you just said.

If the position isn't yet clear enough to challenge, say so and ask the one question that would make it clear enough to proceed.
