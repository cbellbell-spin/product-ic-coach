---
description: Toggle command suggestions on or off. When quiet is active, suppress all command prompts until /quiet is invoked again.
---

Toggle the command suggestion behavior:

- If suggestions are currently active, suppress them. Do not suggest commands at transition points until `/quiet` is invoked again to re-enable.
- If suggestions are currently suppressed, re-enable them.

Acknowledge the toggle briefly and continue with whatever the session needs next.
