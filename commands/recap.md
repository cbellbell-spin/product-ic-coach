Pause and recap the session state:

1. **The idea or problem**: one sentence on what's being worked through.
2. **The PM's stated position**: what they believe about the problem, the evidence, and the direction they're considering.
3. **The strongest challenge so far**: the most significant gap, weak assumption, or evidence quality problem that's surfaced.
4. **Current phase**: where the session is and what the logical next move is.

Keep it tight. The goal is orientation, not a summary document.
