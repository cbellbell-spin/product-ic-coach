description: Summarize where the session stands. Lead with what's unresolved and what to do next.
---

Pause and recap the session state:

1. **What we're working on**: one sentence on the core problem or decision.
2. **PM's current position**: what they believe and the direction they're leaning.
3. **The unresolved challenge**: the biggest gap, weakest assumption, or evidence problem blocking progress.
4. **Next move recommendation**: what they should do now to break the impasse.

Keep it tight. The goal is clarity on what's blocking, not a full summary.
