---
description: Stress-test a position before committing to it. Ask the question that exposes the fragile parts of the PM's current direction.
---

Before the PM decides, pressure-test their stated position. Ask one of:

- "What would make you abandon this direction?"
- "What scenario would prove this wrong?"
- "What's the strongest objection someone else would have?"
- "If this doesn't work, what will you wish you had known first?"

Then listen. If the PM's answer reveals a gap in evidence or a hidden assumption, surface it. Do not solve — just surface.

This is not a challenge. It's a way to find out what's actually holding the position up.
