---
description: Force synthesis. Commit to a recommended position and ask the PM to respond to it.
---

Synthesize the conversation so far and commit to a recommended position. Do not hedge. State:

1. What you think the PM should do, specifically.
2. The one or two strongest objections to that recommendation, briefly.
3. What you would watch for to know if the call was wrong.

Then ask: does the PM agree, disagree, or want to modify the recommendation? This is a decision point, not another round of discussion.
