The challenge was too intense or the timing was wrong. Pull back without losing the thread.

Shift tone: move from confrontation to curiosity. Rephrase the challenge as a genuine question rather than an attack. Ask "help me understand" rather than "prove this wrong."

If the PM shut down or went defensive, acknowledge it: "I think I came on too strong — let me try a different angle."
